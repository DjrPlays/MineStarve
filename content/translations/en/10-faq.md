# Frequently asked questions

Common questions about item use, enchanting requirements and monster spawning.

## Why can I not enchant?

Use a Prestihatitator with compatible equipment or a normal book. Check effective bookshelves, sufficient Nitre, the effective level requirement and the actual levels needed for payment. Enchanting is unavailable beyond 4 distance from the station, while it is burning or when another player is using it.

## Why is power still zero with full bookshelves?

Bookshelves count different book types, not the total number of books. Three identical books are insufficient. Pig Skin and Tentacle Spots recipes make the same book type. Each shelf needs at least three different types, must be within range and must not be burnt.

## Why can a high-level Enchanted Book not transfer?

Check compatibility, conflicts with existing enchantments and whether the transfer would actually improve the item. Equipment sacrifices must match the target's item type. An enchantment already at its cap cannot be upgraded further without another applicable improvement.

## Why does the grindstone not work or refund experience?

Clear the output slot first. Items with only curses, soul binding or protected status cannot undergo ordinary disenchanting. Repair inputs must be two damageable items of the same type. Experience refunds are based on recorded enchanting investment and rounded down.

## Why did my totem not activate?

It must be equipped in the relic slot, not stored in the main inventory or backpack. Equip another after activation. The first-spawn gift is tracked by Klei account within the world, so changing characters or reconnecting does not grant another.

## Why is there no milking action?

Hold an empty Bucket in your hand. The target must be a living adult Beefalo that has completed its 3-day milk cooldown. Holding a Milk Bucket or merely carrying an empty one does not qualify.

## Why do monsters spawn in a lit surface camp?

Surface night spawning is not restricted by light levels. Cave spawn locations must be sufficiently dark.
