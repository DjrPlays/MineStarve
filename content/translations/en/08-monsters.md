# Monsters

Health, attacks, drops and natural spawning rules for Creepers, Skeletons and Zombies.

| Monster | Health | Main threat | Ordinary kill drops |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Creeper | 150 | Triggers at about 4 distance; explosion base damage 140, radius 4; can destroy structures | Gunpowder ×1, with a 30% chance of 1 more; no own drops after self-detonation |
| Skeleton | 300 | Ranged arrows; base damage 30; attack interval 2.5 seconds | Bone Shards ×2 |
| Zombie | 450 | Melee base damage 50; attack interval 2 seconds | Monster Meat ×2 |

## Attacks and drops

A Creeper's explosion damages nearby targets and destroys structures. It drops Gunpowder only if killed before detonating. Skeletons attack with arrows and Zombies use melee attacks. Actual damage varies with armor, enchantments and Seven Curses effects.

## Sunlight and natural spawning

Surface spawning is attempted at night. Caves can attempt spawning at any time, but the spawn location must be sufficiently dark. Locations are generally 12–20 distance from the player, avoiding points within 8 of any player and effective enclosed areas. Surface night spawning is not restricted by light levels.

Skeletons and Zombies take 10 fire damage per second from sunlight on the surface during daytime when there is no rain, snow or shade. Tree shade or an effective canopy, precipitation and caves change these conditions. Creepers do not burn in sunlight.

## Default spawning limits

| Moon phase | Surface daily shared quota per player | Cave daily shared quota per player | Existing population cap per monster type per player |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ordinary phase | 5 | 10 | 5 |
| Full moon | 10 | 20 | 10 |
| New moon | 15 | 30 | 15 |

All three monster types share the daily quota. Death or disconnection does not refund it. Population caps apply separately to each type and scale with the effective player count in the current surface or cave world. Caps do not guarantee spawns: terrain, enclosed areas and spawn conditions still matter. The abundant setting removes daily quotas; surface population caps remain, while cave population caps double.

> The hidden Seven Curses effect allows pursuing hostile creatures to path through walls.
