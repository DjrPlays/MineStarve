# Enchanting and grinding

The Prestihatitator also acts as an enchanting table, and the Cartographer's Desk acts as a grindstone.

## Effective bookshelves

Bookshelves within a radius of 4 turf tiles around the Prestihatitator can add power. Each shelf must contain at least three different types of book. Empty shelves or repeated copies of one type do not count. Ordinary books are distinguished by type; enchanted books by their enchantments. Different enchantment contents can count as different types. Use the interface's effective bookshelf count.

| Effective bookshelves | 1 | 4 | 8 | 12 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Maximum enchanting power | 2 | 10 | 20 | 30 |

Power equals effective bookshelves ×30÷12, rounded down, counting at most 12 shelves. With no effective shelves, power is 0.

## Random enchanting

1. Approach a Prestihatitator and right-click its Enchant action.
2. Put compatible equipment or a normal book in the target slot, and Nitre in the catalyst slot.
3. Check the three options' requirements, costs and previews. They cost 1 level and 1 Nitre, 2 levels and 2 Nitre, or 3 levels and 3 Nitre.
4. Confirm and retrieve the result. A normal book becomes an Enchanted Book; equipment receives compatible enchantments. An ellipsis after the preview indicates that additional enchantments may be included.

## Nine enchantments

| Enchantment | Maximum | Effect | Compatibility |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sharpness | V | +4% ordinary physical damage per level; up to +20% | Weapons; conflicts with Smite |
| Smite | V | +8% ordinary physical damage per level against supported shadow, ghost, undead and similar targets; up to +40% | Weapons; conflicts with Sharpness |
| Looting | III | +10% chance per level to receive one extra ordinary drop | Weapons; maximum base chance 30% |
| Protection | IV | Reduces final combat damage by 4% per level; up to 16% per item | Armor; conflicts with Fire Protection |
| Fire Protection | IV | Reduces burning duration by 20% per level; up to 80% | Armor; conflicts with Protection |
| Unbreaking | III | 12% chance per level to avoid consuming durability; up to 36% | Equipment with uses or armor durability |
| Efficiency | V | +10% tool work efficiency per level; up to +50% | Compatible tools |
| Curse of Binding | I | Cannot be manually unequipped and is not dropped on death | Equippable items; breaking the item can remove it |
| Curse of Vanishing | I | Item disappears when its carrier dies | Compatible enchantable items |

> Binding and Vanishing may appear as hidden additional curses, not as ordinary main previews. Grindstones cannot remove curses. Bound items without durability have a removal mechanism involving sleep induced by the Nightcap. The Ring of Seven Curses' own soul binding is separate from ordinary enchantments. Fire Protection reduces burning duration, not fire damage itself.

## Transferring enchantments

Put the item you want to keep in the target slot. Put an Enchanted Book or enchanted equipment of the same item type in the catalyst slot, then choose Transfer Enchantments. One sacrificial item is consumed, and no Nitre is used. The cost is 1 plus the number of enchantment types already on the target, paid in actual levels. Only compatible, non-conflicting upgrades transfer.

| Combination | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Same enchantment and level | Increases by one level, up to its cap |
| Same enchantment, different levels | Keeps the higher level |
| Conflicts with an existing enchantment | That enchantment cannot transfer |
| Sacrifice is equipment | Must be the same item type as the target |
| Sacrifice has multiple enchantments | Compatible upgrades transfer; the whole sacrifice is consumed on success |

## Disenchanting and repairing

1. Approach a Cartographer's Desk and choose Use Grindstone.
2. For disenchanting, insert one item with ordinary enchantments. For repairs, insert two damageable items of the same type.
3. Choose Process and take the result. Remaining durability is combined, with an extra 5% of maximum durability restored, capped at full durability.

The grindstone removes ordinary enchantments and keeps curses. Soul-bound or protected items cannot be disenchanted. Experience refunds equal 25% of recorded enchanting experience invested, rounded down: these are experience points, not 25% of levels. Combining items for repairs also removes ordinary enchantments.
