# Buckets and milk

Adult Beefalo can periodically produce milk. Drinking a Milk Bucket restores health and sanity and clears temporary effects.

## Milking

1. Craft a Bucket with 2 Flint and 2 Nitre.
2. Equip it in your hand and approach an adult Beefalo that is ready to be milked.
3. Right-click Milk. One empty bucket is consumed and replaced with a Milk Bucket.
4. The same Beefalo takes 3 game days to produce milk again. Young Beefalo cannot be milked.

| Property | Value or result |
| --- | --- |
| Drinking | Health +2, sanity +5, hunger +0 |
| Effect removal | Clears supported temporary effects, including beneficial ones |
| After drinking | Returns the empty Bucket |
| Shelf life at normal temperature | 3 game days |
| Spoilage | 20% chance of Butter, otherwise Rot; also returns the Bucket |

> Milk removes temporary regeneration, fire resistance and absorption granted by a totem. It does not unbind the Ring of Seven Curses or replace Soul Stitching for repairing death stacks. Some temporary effects from other mods may not be removed.
