# Experience and levels

Player levels have no set maximum. Experience mainly comes from eligible kills and personally completing the mining of specific resources.

## Sources of experience

Using a mining tool to finish mining an ordinary boulder, gold-bearing boulder, Moon Rock boulder, flintless boulder or Marble Shrub grants a random 1–2 experience points. Experience is not awarded for every swing, and not every mineable object qualifies. Kill experience depends on the target's maximum health. Kills by projectiles or followers can count when ownership can be traced to the player.

| Target maximum health | Experience per eligible kill |
| --- | --- |
| Below 100 | 1 |
| 100–499 | 3 |
| 500–999 | 10 |
| 1,000–4,999 | 20 |
| 5,000–9,999 | 30 |
| 10,000–19,999 | 50 |
| 20,000 or more | Starts at 50, plus 10 for every full 5,000 health above 20,000 |

## Required levels and spent levels

Random enchanting requires both the option's minimum level and enough actual levels to pay its cost. The three options cost 1, 2 and 3 actual levels, rather than their full minimum requirement. Seven Curses adds 10 effective levels for meeting requirements; those bonus levels cannot be spent.

> At actual level 20 with the Seven Curses bonus of 10, your effective level is 30. This meets a level-30 requirement, while the third option still deducts 3 actual levels. Enchanting power must also support the option.
