# Seven Curses and Enigmatic Legacy

Enigmatic Legacy is disabled by default. Enabling it automatically grants, equips and binds the Ring of Seven Curses, introducing an ongoing set of challenges.

## Seven curses

| Curse | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Suffering | Final combat damage taken ×1.35. |
| Universal hostility | Neutral creatures become hostile; recruitment and taming are restricted. Some creatures, including characters' innate companions, are exempt. |
| Weakened armor | Armor works at 75% effectiveness. For example, 80% absorption becomes 60% before further calculations. |
| Prey's retaliation | Damage dealt ×0.8 against ordinary targets and ×0.7 against bosses. |
| Eternal burning | Default burning duration is 5,000 seconds, with ordinary extinguishing restricted. Fire Protection can shorten it. |
| Soul Fracture | Each death adds a 20% maximum-health penalty, up to three stacks and 60% total. |
| Sleeplessness | Normal sleep is prevented, and sanity loss at night or in dark caves doubles. |

## Blessings and benefits

| Blessing | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Fortune and misfortune | Luck +1. |
| Plunder | Grants Looting I. Combined with weapon Looting, the maximum effective level is IV, for up to 40% extra ordinary drop chance. |
| Sustained by battle | Eligible hostile kills heal 1 health for ordinary targets, 5 for large targets and 15 for bosses. |
| Forbidden infusion | +10 effective enchanting levels; these are not actual spendable levels. |
| Cursed gifts | Enables Seven Curses exclusive drops. |
| Shadow companion | Access storage shared with Maxwell's Magician's Chest from the inventory. |
| Master of fate | Enables crafting, equipping and triggering Seven Curses relics. |

## Eternal burning and Soul Fractures

Eternal burning lasts 5,000 seconds by default. Reaching 35 wetness or becoming frozen can extinguish it; some water-spraying effects also work. To repair a death stack, craft Soul Stitching near a qualifying Ancient tier 2 station. Each use consumes 1 Life Giving Amulet and 4 Nightmare Fuel to repair one stack.

## Hidden curses and binding

While hidden curses are active, knockback distance taken is multiplied by 1.5, and affected hostile creatures can path through walls when pursuing. Ordinary unequipping, grinding and drinking milk cannot unbind the Ring of Seven Curses.
