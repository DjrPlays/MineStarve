# Oak boats

Small boats with two oars provide ocean travel. Chest boats add storage. Both must be deployed in the water near shore before boarding.

| Boat | Health | Materials and features |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Oak Boat | 800 | Boards ×3 + Tentacle Spots ×1; basic boat with two oars |
| Oak Chest Boat | 1,000 | Boards ×6 + Tentacle Spots ×1; includes 12 storage slots |

## Rowing

1. Deploy the boat item in water near shore and jump aboard.
2. Click the boat and choose Row to take control.
3. Use movement keys to steer, or left-click a destination to row there.
4. Right-click to stop rowing. On a controller, use Cancel.
5. Toggle chest boat storage through the boat status display. Opening storage stops rowing.

> Boats have limited health and drop their stored contents when they sink. Only one player can row a boat at a time.
