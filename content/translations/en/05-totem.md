# Totem of Undying

When equipped in the relic slot, a totem prevents one lethal hit and is consumed.

## Obtaining and triggering

Each Klei account receives 1 totem on its first spawn in that world. Reconnecting, changing characters or travelling between the surface and caves does not grant another. Each Rabbit King weapon given to the Pig King yields 2 totems. The Pig King still refuses merm characters.

| Effect | Value and duration |
| --- | --- |
| Survival | Consumes the equipped totem and sets health to 1 |
| Brief invulnerability | 1 second, blocking immediate follow-up damage |
| Fire resistance | 40 seconds |
| Regeneration | 2 health every 1.25 seconds for 45 seconds |
| Damage absorption | 60 points for 10 seconds |

> The totem clears temporary effects before granting its own. Immediate health after activation is 1. Spare totems in the inventory do not automatically count as equipped.
