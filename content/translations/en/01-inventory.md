# Inventory and equipment

The inventory includes main slots, backpack slots, equipment slots and accessory slots.

| Area | Rules |
| --- | --- |
| Main inventory | 45 slots by default; configurable to 30 or the original 15. The original hotbar retains 15 slots. |
| Backpack | Uses a separate equipment slot by default. MC mode also allows backpacks containing items to be placed in the player inventory. |
| Clothing | With extra equipment slots enabled, supported vanilla clothing uses a separate slot and can be worn with body armor. Not all body equipment counts as clothing. |
| Enlightened Crown | Uses a separate slot when extra equipment slots are enabled; its effects remain active. |
| Accessories | Includes a spellstone slot, two ring slots, an amulet slot and a relic slot. Each item needs a compatible slot. |
| Amulets | Supports the six vanilla gem amulets. |
| Relics | Equip a Totem of Undying here to activate it. |
| Extended slots | Can display supported equipment from other mods. A setting controls whether Capability Medal equipment also appears in the original slots. |

## Opening and closing

Open the interface through the game's self-inspection action. Close it with Cancel or the inventory key. Keys follow your in-game control bindings.

> When Seven Curses is enabled, the bound Ring of Seven Curses occupies the first ring slot.
